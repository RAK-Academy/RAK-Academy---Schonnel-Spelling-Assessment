Assessment Portal Guidelines

Please read these general guidelines before beginning your assigned tasks.

  • 🔑School Email: Sign in using your school email to access your personalised student dashboard.
  • 📋Spelling Test: Your teacher will tell you when the assessment window is open.
  • ⏱️Timer & Focus: The spelling test is timed and will monitor tab focus. Close all other tabs before starting.
  • 💾Auto-save: Your progress is saved automatically throughout the test.
  • 🛡️Independent Work: Complete the assessment independently. Copying, pasting or leaving the active screen is recorded.
🔒 Assessment results are stored securely and reviewed by authorised school staff.
